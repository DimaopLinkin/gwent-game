# Gwent Deck Building Rules - The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

**Master Reference**: [02_game_specifications.md](./02_game_specifications.md)

This document describes all deck construction rules and requirements for Witcher 3 Gwent.

---

## Deck Construction Requirements

### Minimum Deck Size
- **Minimum**: 25 cards
- **Reason**: Ensures sufficient variety and doesn't create degenerate strategies
- **Recommendation**: 30-35 cards for optimal consistency

### Maximum Deck Size
- **Maximum**: Typically unlimited (or very high limit like 60+)
- **Practical Maximum**: 40-45 cards (point of diminishing returns)
- **Larger Decks**: Reduce consistency, increase variance

### Recommended Deck Size
- **Optimal**: 30-35 cards
- **Reason**: Balance between consistency and variety
- **Trade-off**: Fewer cards = more consistent draws, but less flexibility

## Faction Requirements

### Primary Faction
- **Primary Faction**: Most cards must be from chosen faction (60-80% typical)
- **Faction Lock**: Choosing faction locks in leader choice
- **Faction Identity**: Factions have distinct card pools and mechanics

### Faction-Specific Cards
- **Northern Realms**: Northern Realms cards only
- **Nilfgaard**: Nilfgaard cards only
- **Skellige**: Skellige cards only
- **Scoia'tael**: Scoia'tael (Elves) cards only
- **Monsters**: Monster cards only

### Cross-Faction Cards
- **Limited Crossover**: Some cards available across multiple factions
- **Neutral Cards**: Certain universal cards (if available)
- **Restrictions**: Most cards are faction-locked

### Leader Binding
- **Leader Lock**: Selected leader determines faction
- **One Leader**: One leader per deck
- **Faction Match**: Leader must match deck's primary faction

## Card Limits

### Copy Limits
Most Witcher 3 Gwent implementations use copy limits:

**Typical Limits**:
- **Gold/Legendary**: 1 copy per deck (unique, iconic cards)
- **Silver/Rare**: 2 copies per deck
- **Bronze/Common**: 3 copies per deck
- **Neutral**: Same limits if available

**Exceptions**:
- Some implementations may allow more flexible limits
- Tournament rules may vary
- Specific cards may have unique restrictions

### Card Restrictions
- **Faction Exclusivity**: Card can only be used in its faction
- **Copy Limit**: Cannot exceed limit for card's rarity
- **Unique Cards**: Some legendary cards have unique restrictions
- **Seasonal**: Some cards may be restricted by game mode/season

## Card Type Distribution

### Recommended Distribution (Example)
For a 30-card deck:

**By Type**:
- **Units**: 18-22 cards (60-75%)
  - Mix of vanilla and ability cards
  - Balanced across zones
- **Special Cards**: 4-6 cards (13-20%)
  - Horns, Decoys, Spies
  - Faction-specific effects
- **Weather**: 2-4 cards (7-13%)
  - Rain, Fog, Frost
  - Strategic utility

**By Zone**:
- **Close Combat**: 8-10 cards
- **Ranged**: 8-10 cards
- **Siege**: 6-8 cards
- **Special/Heroes**: 4-6 cards

**By Ability**:
- **Vanilla Units**: 40-50%
  - Plain strength contributors
- **Ability Cards**: 40-50%
  - Cards with effects (Spy, Decoy, etc.)
- **Weather/Special**: 10-20%
  - Environmental effects

### Distribution Guidelines

**Balanced Deck**:
- Equal strength across zones
- Mix of low, medium, high strength units
- Combination of abilities and vanilla cards
- Weather coverage

**Specialized Deck**:
- Heavy focus on one faction mechanic
- Example: Skellige resilience-heavy
- Requires careful synergy planning
- May be stronger in matchups but weaker in others

## Deck Building Strategy

### Card Selection Process

1. **Choose Faction**: Select primary faction
2. **Choose Leader**: Pick leader matching faction
3. **Select Core Cards**: Must-include high-value cards
4. **Add Synergy Cards**: Cards that work with core
5. **Fill Remaining Slots**: Balance and consistency cards
6. **Test and Refine**: Adjust based on meta

### Synergy Building

**Within-Faction Synergy**:
- Northern Realms: Commander effects stack
- Nilfgaard: Spies + draws create advantage
- Skellige: Resilience + medics enable recycling
- Scoia'tael: Agents + positioning
- Monsters: Devouring combinations

**Cross-Card Synergy**:
- Muster cards with multiple copies
- Horns with high-unit zones
- Weather with opponent's weak zones
- Medics with resilient cards

### Meta Considerations

**Against Specific Opponents**:
- Northern Realms: Build resilience or disruption
- Nilfgaard: Protect against spies and draws
- Skellige: Focus on removal or speed
- Scoia'tael: Adapt to positioning
- Monsters: Counter devouring with removal

**Environmental Factors**:
- Weather-heavy decks → include weather counters
- Spell-heavy decks → include resilience
- Aggressive decks → include medics
- Control decks → include counter-effects

## Advanced Deck Building

### Muster-Heavy Deck
- Include many muster cards
- Multiple copies deploy at once
- Creates explosive turns
- Example: 3x Infantry + 3x Archer + 3x Soldier = rapid deployment

### Resilience-Heavy Deck
- Focus on Skellige resilience
- Cards return for reuse
- Long-term value strategy
- Example: Multiple resilient units + medics

### Control Deck
- Heavy disruption and removal
- Opponent interaction focus
- Defensive positioning
- Example: Spies + weather + removal effects

### Aggressive Deck
- High-value units
- Early pressure
- Minimized removal options
- Example: High-strength cards, few specials

### Combo Deck
- Synergy-dependent
- Requires specific card combinations
- High skill ceiling
- Example: Devouring combos + monster synergy
